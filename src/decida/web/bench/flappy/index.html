Flappy

the model says where the bird is; the game flaps when P(below) clears the threshold
Press Start. Each tick the game puts the bird's height into words and asks one question.
0.50

The words variant follows the Laya playground's Flappy: the game measures, the model only reads ("the bird is a little below the gap") and answers where the bird is. Encoders do this well. numbers and action show what goes wrong: they cannot compare digits, and asking what to do comes out inverted.
