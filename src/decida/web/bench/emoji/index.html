Emoji finder

type what you are looking for; the model picks the emoji that match, one yes/no each

Your text is the state and every emoji is judged separately by a short statement ("A glove is an example of this."), so several can match, none can, and the model's certainty shows as a percentage under the floating ones. A model that answers about 50% for everything is not telling emoji apart: watch "Median answer" and "Best minus median". Hover an emoji to see its name. Small models often have no idea; a large one usually does.
